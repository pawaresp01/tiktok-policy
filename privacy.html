Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

AutoFlywheel ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This application is designed as an internal automation tool for content creators.

Information We Collect

We only request the minimum required permissions from the TikTok API: user.info.basic (to verify your identity) and video.upload (to push video files to your draft folder).

How We Use Your Data

We do not store your TikTok passwords, personal data, or video content on any external servers. All operations are processed locally on your system. We do not publish videos directly; all content is strictly sent to your TikTok Drafts for your manual review.
