Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

AutoFlywheel respects your privacy. We only request the minimum required permissions (user.info.basic and video.upload) to push video files to your draft folder. We do not store your TikTok passwords, and we do not publish videos without your explicit consent within the TikTok app. By using this internal tool, you agree to comply with TikTok's community guidelines.
